SQUELCH v0.8 — acid bass synth

INSTALL (VST3)
1. Close FL Studio.
2. Copy the whole "Squelch.vst3" folder into:
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (replace the old one if it's there)
3. Open FL Studio -> Options -> Manage plugins -> Find more plugins (or "Start scan").
4. Squelch appears under Generators.

QUICK TEST WITHOUT A DAW
Double-click Squelch.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC",
click More info -> Run anyway. Set your audio device under Options, then play your MIDI keyboard.

PLAYING IT LIKE ACID
- Overlapping notes = SLIDE. In FL's piano roll, make a note run into the next one.
- Velocity 100 or higher = ACCENT (louder, filter jumps open).
- The squelch lives in Cutoff, Resonance and Env Mod. Turn Resonance up to 70%+.
- Several accents in a row with high resonance build up — that's intended.

THE INTERFACE
- Click the smiley logo (top left) to switch theme: Phosphor Lab -> Matrix -> Kawaii.
- Arrows next to the preset name step through presets; click the name for the full list.
  (The 10 presets are starters and will be retuned once the core sound is final.)
- Waves: SAW / SQUARE / PULSE (PW knob = pulse width) / SUPER (knob becomes DETUNE).
- Drive FX: the round light turns it on/off. OD = warm, DIST = dense, FUZZ = harsh.
  TONE = dark..bright, LEVEL = drive output, MIX = dry..wet.
- OUT bar: shows output level. Drag the white handle to set the volume.
- Double-click any knob to reset it. Drag the window's bottom-right corner to resize.

SEQ PAGE (v0.4)
Click SEQ at the top to switch pages. Choose a mode:
- OFF: your keyboard plays the synth directly (as before).
- PATTERN: plays the 16 steps. Three ways to start it:
  * hold a key on your keyboard or a note in the piano roll: it plays from step 1 in that key
    (middle C = as written; FL calls it C5) and stops when you let go
  * press PLAY: runs on its own
  Inside FL the tempo is always FL's (the BPM box shows it, e.g. "140 FL").
  The BPM box only sets the tempo in the standalone app.
  * start FL: follows FL's tempo; keys then transpose it
- ARP: hold notes on your keyboard and it arpeggiates them.

Editing steps:
- NOTE: drag up/down or use the mouse wheel. Double-click resets to C.
- OCT: click to cycle (- / UP / DN).
- ACC, SLIDE, ON: click to toggle; click and drag across steps to set several.
- LENGTH sets how many steps loop (odd lengths give rolling patterns).

ARP controls: MODE (Up, Down, Up-Down, Random, As Played), OCTAVE (1-4),
ACCENT (Off, every 2/3/4/5/7 steps, or Auto), SLIDE (how often notes glide),
LATCH (keeps playing after you let go), CHORD (one key = a chord from KEY + SCALE).
RATE, SWING and GATE apply to both PATTERN and ARP.

PRINT TO STEPS copies what the arp is playing into the pattern.
DRAG MIDI: drag it into FL's piano roll, channel rack or playlist to drop the pattern in as MIDI.

GENERATE / MUTATE (v0.5)
- GENERATE writes a new acid line into the 16 steps, using the KEY and SCALE knobs.
  It follows classic acid habits (repeating motif, root-heavy, octave jumps, a few rests,
  accents and slides placed musically) instead of random notes. Click again for another.
- MUTATE changes 2-3 steps of the current line and keeps the rest (step 1 stays put).
- Both switch the sequencer to PATTERN so you hear the result.

FX PAGE (v0.6)
Click FX at the top.
- MOVEMENT (TD-3-MO style extras):
  SOFT ATK  attack of normal notes (accented notes always hit hard)
  ACC DECAY how long the filter stays open on accented notes
  TRACKING  cutoff follows the note, so high notes get brighter
  FILTER FM the oscillator wobbles the filter: growl and grit
  SWEEP     how fast a run of accents winds the filter open (Slow / Normal / Fast)
- DELAY: ping-pong echo synced to FL's tempo (or the BPM knob). Red light = on/off.
  TIME (3/16 = classic acid echo), FEEDBACK, TONE (dark..bright), MIX.
- REVERB: SIZE, DAMP, MIX. Red light = on/off.

YOUR OWN PRESETS
- Click the preset name -> "Save current sound...", type a name, press Enter.
- Your presets appear as U1, U2... after the factory ones, in the menu and with the arrows.
- "Open presets folder" in the same menu shows where they live
  (C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\Squelch\Presets). Delete or share the files there.
- Presets store the sound (synth, movement, drive, delay, reverb), not the sequencer pattern.

NEW IN v0.8
- Three new themes (click the smiley logo to cycle): PHOSPHOR LAB (default: beige lab
  plastic in pink/purple light, green CRT), MATRIX (black + neon green, code rain),
  KAWAII (white + pink, laptop stickers).
- The filter screen is live: the curve follows the envelope with phosphor trails, and
  CH2 shows the real output waveform. It only moves when sound is playing.
- Knob values, step notes and boxes are shown on little green (or pink) displays.
- The footer shows MIDI / SEQ status, the output meter and the volume handle.

NEW IN v0.7
- New interface, matching Aymane's mockup. Click the smiley logo to cycle themes:
  (replaced by the v0.8 themes).
- Values like LENGTH, RATE, BPM, KEY, SCALE, TIME are boxes: click the arrows,
  drag up/down, scroll, or click the value for a list.
- SEQ page in ARP mode shows the arp controls in place of the step grid.
- DELAY: PING-PONG (off = centred echoes) and SYNC (off = free time in ms).
- REVERB: PRE-DELAY (gap before the reverb), LOW CUT (no muddy bass in the reverb),
  DUCKING (reverb dips while notes play and blooms in the gaps).
- OUT meter: the white line is the volume; drag it.
