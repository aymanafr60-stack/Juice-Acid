SQUELCH v0.1 — acid bass voice

INSTALL (VST3)
1. Close FL Studio.
2. Copy the whole "Squelch.vst3" folder into:
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
3. Open FL Studio -> Options -> Manage plugins -> Find more plugins (or "Start scan").
4. Squelch appears under Generators.

QUICK TEST WITHOUT A DAW
Double-click Squelch.exe. Set your audio device in the Options button at the top.
Click the on-screen keys, or plug in the MiniLab.

PLAYING IT LIKE ACID
- Overlapping notes = SLIDE. In FL's piano roll, make a note run into the next one.
- Velocity 100 or higher = ACCENT (louder, filter jumps open).
  On the on-screen keyboard, where you click on a key sets the velocity.
- The squelch lives in Cutoff, Resonance and Env Mod. Turn Resonance up to 70%+.
- Several accents in a row with high resonance build up — that's intended.
